About

Hi, I'm Devon.

I'm a data analyst in Halifax, Nova Scotia. I've been working in data for six years: Tableau dashboards at Kinduct, supply chain KPIs at Irving Shipbuilding, and now Snowflake and more SQL than I'd like to admit at Dentalcorp. I also can't stop building things after hours.

Away from a screen, you'll usually find me at CrossFit, training for Hyrox or out on a run. Otherwise I'm with my partner, Lauren, and my dog, Gus. I travel whenever I can, and my last big trip took me through Italy, England and Madeira.

My reading is all over the place. This year's favourites: Capote's In Cold Blood, Pratchett's The Colour of Magic, Édouard Levé's Suicide and Murakami's What I Talk About When I Talk About Running. I've also torn through eight Dungeon Crawler Carl books, because once you start, it's hard to stop.
